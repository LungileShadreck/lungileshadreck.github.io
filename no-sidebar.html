Social Media Marketing

Building online presence, growing audiences, and driving real engagement — across the platforms that matter.

Role: SMM Strategist + Content Creator Instagram · Facebook · LinkedIn · TikTok 2021 — Present

The Platforms

Social media isn't one-size-fits-all. Each platform has its own content language, algorithmic behaviour, and audience expectations — and effective strategy means adapting to all of them:

  • Instagram — Visual storytelling, Reels, carousels, and story-driven branded content
  • Facebook — Community building, event promotion, and targeted advertising posts
  • LinkedIn — Professional positioning, thought leadership, and B2B content
  • TikTok — Short-form video, trending audio integration, and brand personality
  • Twitter / X — Real-time engagement, announcements, and brand voice refinement

Full-Service Approach

Social media marketing isn't just posting — it's about building a consistent brand presence that people recognise, trust, and engage with. The work covers the full cycle:

  • Content calendars and strategic posting schedules aligned to business goals
  • Branded graphic creation for posts, stories, and reels
  • Caption writing and copy with a calibrated tone of voice
  • Marketing campaign planning and execution
  • Audience growth strategies and community management
  • Performance tracking and basic analytics reporting

Real Experience

The first formal SMM role came from within a graphic design position at a private company. After consistently delivering strong visual content, the role expanded to managing the company's full social media presence — from strategy through to execution. The work included running promotional campaigns, growing follower counts, and ensuring all social content aligned with the company's rebranded visual identity.

Since then, the work has extended to helping small businesses and entrepreneurs across Francistown establish their digital footprint — connecting them with their target audiences and building the kind of online presence that generates real commercial results.

What Sets This Apart

  • Social strategy informed by graphic design expertise — the content looks as good as it performs
  • Deep understanding of both local (Botswana) and international audience dynamics
  • Integrated approach — social content is designed to complement web presence and brand identity
  • Culturally literate content that resonates with African markets while remaining globally relevant
